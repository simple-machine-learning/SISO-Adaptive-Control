Mechanical: voice-coil servo¶

Python model: plant_models.voice_coil_servo

Description¶

Electromechanical position servo driven by a voice-coil actuator. The model contains amplifier dynamics, coil resistance and inductance, back electromotive force, carriage inertia, suspension stiffness, viscous damping, smooth Coulomb friction, and a cubic restoring term.

Model equations¶

State variables¶

The physical state vector is

\[\mathbf{x}(t)=[x(t),\,v(t),\,i(t),\,V_a(t)]^\mathsf{T}.\]

Static and auxiliary relations¶

The amplifier command and mechanical forces are

\[\begin{split}\begin{aligned} V_{cmd}(t) &= K_a \cdot \tanh(u(t)), \\ F_{em}(t) &= K_t \cdot i(t), \\ F_f(t) &= c \cdot v(t) + F_c \cdot \tanh\!\left(\frac{v(t)}{v_s}\right), \\ F_s(t) &= k \cdot x(t) + k_3 \cdot x^3(t). \end{aligned}\end{split}\]

State equations¶

The implemented continuous-time dynamics are

\[\begin{split}\begin{aligned} \dot{x}(t) &= v(t), \\ m \cdot \dot{v}(t) &= F_{em}(t)-F_f(t)-F_s(t), \\ L \cdot \dot{i}(t) &= V_a(t)-R \cdot i(t)-K_e \cdot v(t), \\ \tau_a \cdot \dot{V}_a(t) &= V_{cmd}(t)-V_a(t). \end{aligned}\end{split}\]

Output equation¶

The controlled input and output used by the identification and control algorithms are defined explicitly as

\[\begin{split}\begin{aligned} u(t)\in\mathbb{R}\quad\text{(drive-voltage command)},\\ y(t)=x(t). \end{aligned}\end{split}\]

Parameter implementation¶

The editable default parameters are defined in apps/simulated/plant_models/voice_coil_servo.py. They are fields of PlantParams near the beginning of that file. The function default_params() returns the default parameter object used by the GUI and simulation.
